### Course Information
- **Course Title:** CS 34800: Information Systems

### Students With Disabilities

Purdue University strives to make learning experiences as accessible as possible. If you anticipate or experience physical or academic barriers due to a disability, you are encouraged to reach out to the Disability Resource Center at:

- Email: drc@purdue.edu
- Phone: 765-494-1247

Students are also welcome to inform the lead TA to discuss options. If you have a DRC recommendation letter and cannot participate in hotseat questions in class, please communicate this. For those with exam-time extensions, exams must be scheduled with the DRC testing center a few weeks before the exam date.

### Mental Health Statement

If you are starting to feel stress, anxiety, or are overwhelmed, consider using WellTrack for support. Sign in to access a variety of tools and information. For additional support and resources, visit the Office of the Dean of Students during drop-in hours (M-F, 8 am-5 pm). Purdue University is dedicated to promoting the mental health and well-being of its students. If you or someone you know is feeling overwhelmed, depressed, or in need of mental health support, services are available. To seek help, contact Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS) at 765-494-6995 at any time, including after hours, weekends, and holidays. You can also visit the CAPS office located on the second floor of the Purdue University Student Health Center (PUSH) during regular business hours.
