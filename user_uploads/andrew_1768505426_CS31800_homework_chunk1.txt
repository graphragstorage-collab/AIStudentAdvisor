**Computer Science Course Homework Assignments CS 381 - Spring 2026**
###
This document contains the homework assignments for the CS381 computer science course for spring 2026

### Key Topics and Concepts
- Algorithm design and analysis
- Problem-solving strategies, including identifying subproblems and changing input
- Proofs of correctness, including reductions and induction
- Multiple choice questions and exercises from various chapters and sections

The course consists of several homework assignments, each with its own set of problems and exercises. The assignments are due on specific dates and times, and students must submit their work through the Gradescope platform. The assignments cover topics such as algorithm design, problem-solving strategies, and proofs of correctness.
