CS 34800: Information Systems Syllabus - Spring 2026

### Course Information
- **Course Title:** CS 34800: Information Systems
- **CRNs:** 13399 and 35118
- **Instructional Modality:** Face-to-Face
- **Course Credit Hours:** 3
- **Prerequisites:** Data Structures, Discrete Structures, and programming experience.

Homework and Exam Policies

### General Guidelines
- Private questions are allowed, but for faster responses, post questions on Ed where multiple TAs can see them.
- Avoid using email for homework or exam inquiries; utilize Gradescope for regrade requests for assignments submitted there.
- Contact the lead TA for general issues regarding assignments.

### Homework Expectations
- Six graded homework assignments will be given, each with submission instructions.
- Homework must be typed using LaTeX, MS Word, or any comparable text editor. Only PDF formats are accepted on Gradescope.
- **Late Homework Policy:** 
  - Late homework is accepted within 72 hours of the due date.
  - A penalty of 0.25 points per day will be imposed.
  - You have five extension days which can be used across any assignments.
  
### Regrade Requests
- Submit regrade requests within three days of receiving graded assignments.
- Valid requests must demonstrate careful review of the posted solution and include a clear explanation for the regrade.
- Note: Regrade requests may result in a lower grade if deemed appropriate.

### Project
- One individual application project will take place over the semester, involving the development of a small database-backed website. Further details will be provided later.

### Quizzes
- Weekly quizzes (typically on Sundays) will be posted in Brightspace and are due within a week.
- You may take each quiz up to three times; the highest score will be considered.
- Quiz formats include multiple-choice, True/False, and Multi-select. For multi-select questions, grades are calculated based on the number of correct answers minus incorrect answers.
- Quizzes are auto-graded, and you will see your score immediately after submission.

### Attendance and Class Participation
- Class attendance will not be recorded, but participation in IClicker Cloud multiple-choice questions contributes 3% to your course grade.
- Participation through IClicker is limited to students present in class; violations will lead to a zero grade for participation.
- You may discuss IClicker questions with classmates before submission. Incorrect answers receive partial credit (usually 85%).
- The lowest 25% of your IClicker responses will be dropped to accommodate missed classes.
  
### Technical Issues
- If you experience technical issues with IClicker, email the instructor with:
  - Date of the lecture
  - Question number and your selected option
  - Screenshot of the error encountered

### Exams
- Two midterm evening exams will be conducted, open-book and open-note.
- Electronic devices are prohibited during exams.
- Make-up exams are only allowed for fully documented illnesses or emergencies.
- A lab exam will cover content from Assignments 1 and 2 (SQL) and will be scheduled during weeks 7 and 8, also open-book and open-note.

### Grade Calculations
- **Final Grades Breakdown:**
  - Assignments: 10%
  - Brightspace Quizzes: 2%
  - In-class Participation: 3%
  - Lab Exam (SQL): 20%
  - 1st Midterm Exam: 25%
  - 2nd Midterm Exam: 30%
  - Web/Mobile Application Project: 10%

### AI and LLM Tools Policy
- The use of AI and LLM tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Google Bard) is prohibited in homework, quizzes, iClicker questions, written exams, and lab exams, but allowed for the application project. Changes to this policy will be communicated.

### Netiquette Guidelines
- Follow netiquette rules for online communication:
  - Be respectful and reflective of what others say.
  - Avoid using all caps, which is perceived as shouting.
  - No offensive language and be clear in communication.
  - Allow for mistakes by others and maintain a collegial dialogue.

### Academic Integrity Statement
- Purdue University enforces strict policies on academic integrity. All assignments are checked for plagiarism. Violations lead to course failure and further consequences. 
- Posting homework questions or solutions online is prohibited.

### Nondiscrimination Statement
- Purdue University is dedicated to fostering a respectful and inclusive environment for every individual.
