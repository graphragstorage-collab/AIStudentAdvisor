- **Major Climate Impacts**:
  - Global temperature rise of approximately 1.2 °C.
  - Increase in heatwaves and shifting seasons.
  - Polar ice melt and glacial retreat.
  - Sea-level rise of approximately 20 cm.
  - Coastal erosion and increased frequency/intensity of extreme weather events.

### Climate Change Impacts

#### Hurricanes and Typhoons
Warmer ocean temperatures can intensify hurricanes and typhoons, leading to more destructive storms. Coastal regions face heightened risks of storm surge and flooding, necessitating early warning systems and resilient infrastructure.

#### Droughts
Increased temperatures and changing precipitation patterns contribute to more frequent and severe droughts. This has adverse effects on agriculture, water supply, and ecosystems, particularly in arid regions, leading to food and water shortages and exacerbating conflicts.

#### Flooding
Heavy rainfall events are increasing in frequency, resulting in greater flooding, especially in urban areas with poor drainage. Effective flood management strategies include improved drainage systems, green infrastructure, and floodplain restoration.

#### Ocean Acidification
The rise in atmospheric CO₂ levels leads to higher concentrations in oceans, causing increased acidity that harms marine life, especially organisms with calcium carbonate shells, such as corals and shellfish.

#### Coral Reefs
Highly sensitive to temperature and acidity changes, coral reefs suffer from bleaching and mortality due to ocean acidification and warming waters, threatening biodiversity. Protecting and restoring coral reefs is vital for marine conservation.

#### Marine Ecosystems
Acidification disrupts the health and survival of various marine species, affecting food webs and ecosystems, with implications for commercial fisheries and coastal communities. Efforts to reduce CO₂ emissions and enhance marine protected areas are crucial.

### Chapter 4: Mitigation and Adaptation

Addressing climate change requires a dual approach: **mitigation** to reduce emissions and **adaptation** to make necessary adjustments to minimize damage.

#### Renewable Energy
Transitioning to renewable energy sources such as wind, solar, and hydroelectric power is essential for reducing greenhouse gas emissions. These sources produce little to no emissions and are sustainable.

- **Solar Power**: Utilizing solar energy through photovoltaic cells or solar thermal systems. Technological advancements and decreasing costs enhance its accessibility.
- **Wind Power**: Generates electricity from wind turbines and is rapidly growing, with offshore wind farms providing significant expansion potential.
- **Hydroelectric Power**: Generates electricity from flowing water but may impact river ecosystems, requiring balance between energy needs and ecological conservation.

#### Energy Efficiency
Improving energy efficiency across buildings, transportation, and industry can drastically lower emissions.

- **Building Efficiency**: Energy-efficient buildings reduce demands for heating, cooling, and lighting, achieved through enhanced insulation and energy-efficient systems.
- **Transportation Efficiency**: Fuel-efficient vehicles, public transport, and electric vehicle infrastructure are critical for reducing transportation emissions.
- **Industrial Efficiency**: Upgrading machinery and processes enhances efficiency, reduces emissions, and lowers costs.

#### Reforestation and Afforestation
Planting trees and restoring forests can absorb CO₂, with reforestation focusing on degraded lands and afforestation on previously unforested areas. Community involvement and sustainable practices are vital for success.

#### Climate Policy
Effective climate policy drives large-scale change, incorporating:
- **International Agreements**: Frameworks like the Paris Agreement set targets for emission reductions globally.
- **National Policies**: Countries develop strategies such as carbon pricing and renewable energy incentives aligned with climate goals.
- **Local Initiatives**: Local governments engage in urban planning and conservation efforts to implement climate actions.

#### Research and Innovation
Continuous research fosters new technologies and strategies to combat climate change, including renewable energy and sustainable agriculture. Collaboration among governments, industries, and academia is essential.
