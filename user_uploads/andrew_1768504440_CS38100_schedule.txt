**Purdue University CS381: Fundamental Algorithms Spring 2026**
===========================================================

The CS381 course at Purdue University, taught by instructors Mike Atallah and Kent Quanrud, covers fundamental algorithms in computer science. The course is offered in the Spring 2026 semester and includes lectures, office hours, and homework assignments.

### Key Topics and Schedule
The course covers a range of topics, including:

- Searching and sorting algorithms
- Induction and recursion
- Graph algorithms, including depth-first search and breadth-first search
- NP-Completeness and optimization problems
- Network flow and disjoint paths
- Randomized algorithms and data structures

The course schedule includes:

- **Lectures:** Tuesday and Thursday, 10:30-11:45 AM (SMTH 108) and 1:30-2:45 PM (BHEE 170)
- **Office hours:** Various times and locations, including LWSN 2116D and LWSN Commons
- **Homework assignments:** Due weekly, starting January 22
- **Midterm exams:** February 18 and April 1
- **Final exam:** TBD
