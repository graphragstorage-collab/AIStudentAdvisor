### Course Description
Introduction to discrete mathematics tools for computer science including:
- Logic and proof methods
- Functions and sets
- Number theory
- Algorithms and growth functions
- Induction and recursion
- Counting and probability
- Trees and graphs
- Finite-state automata, regular expressions
- Context-free languages, Turing machines, and computability

### Learning Outcomes
- Apply discrete-math principles
- Analyze algorithm complexity (Big O, Θ, Ω)
- Solve graph-theory problems

### Required Text
