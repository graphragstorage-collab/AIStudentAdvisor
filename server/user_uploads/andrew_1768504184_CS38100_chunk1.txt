**CS 381: Fundamental Algorithms Course Overview**

The CS 381 course, offered by the Computer Science department, focuses on the study of fundamental algorithms. This undergraduate course covers a range of topics, including algorithm design and analysis, and is similar to the Spring 2025 course available at [Fundamental Algorithms Course](https://fundamentalalgorithms.com/fas24). The course utilizes various resources, such as lecture notes, textbooks, and online materials, to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of algorithms.

###

**Key Resources and Textbooks**

The following resources are provided to supplement the course:

- **Lecture Notes**
  - Usually one chapter per lecture.

- **Recommended Textbooks**
  - Jeff Erickson's "Algorithms" (2019)
  - Jon M. Kleinberg and Éva Tardos' "Algorithm Design" (2006)
  - Sanjoy Dasgupta, Christos H. Papadimitriou, and Umesh V. Vazirani's "Algorithms" (2008)
  - Thomas H. Cormen, Charles E. Leiserson, Ronald L. Rivest, and Clifford Stein's "Introduction to Algorithms" (2009)

- **Online Resources**
  - Video lectures from MIT's 6.006 and 6.046 courses.

###

**Course Structure and Policies**

- **Components**
  - The course consists of lectures, homework assignments, and exams.

- **Homework Assignments**
  - Due weekly, with specified late policy.

- **Exams**
  - Midterms and a final, consisting of multiple-choice and word problems.

- **Grading**
  - Grades are curved with the following weight distribution:
    - Homework: 25%
    - Midterm 1: 20%
    - Midterm 2: 25%
    - Final: 30%

- **Collaboration Policy**
  - Collaboration is allowed; students are encouraged to interact with each other and instructors during office hours and through EdStem.

###

**Grading and Assessment**

- **Homework Submissions**
  - Collected online through Gradescope.
  - Word problems are graded, with the bottom one-fifth of scores dropped.
  - Late submissions accepted with a penalty of 35% of the total points.
  - Exemplary submissions may be selected for solutions, with 10% extra credit awarded.
  - Regrade requests must be initiated within one week of grades being returned.

**Exams and Homework Policies**

- **Exams**
  - Each exam will include multiple-choice questions and word problems, with extended time allocated.

- **Homework**
  - Due every week by Thursday at 11:59 PM.
  - Submissions should be typeset in LaTeX or equivalent formats.
  
- **Collaboration and Group Work**
  - Collaboration among students is encouraged, with up to three students per submission. All collaborators must be indicated.

- **Late Submission Policy**
  - Submissions can be accepted up to 4 days late with a 35% penalty on the total points.

- **Exemplary Solutions**
  - Selected submissions will be published as solutions, with anonymity options available.

- **Regrades and Resubmissions**
  - Regrade requests must be made within a week. Students can utilize released solutions for later submissions, citing them appropriately.

- **Typesetting**
  - Submissions must adhere to mathematical and scientific formatting standards, ideally using LaTeX.
