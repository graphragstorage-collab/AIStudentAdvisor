Fall 2026 Course Offerings for ECE, Data Science, and Mathematics at Purdue University, West Lafayette

This document outlines the Fall 2026 course offerings for the Electrical & Computer Engineering (ECE) department, Data Science (DS) program, and Mathematics (MA) courses at Purdue University, West Lafayette. As of April 2026, the Elmore Family School of ECE has not publicly released a course list with instructor assignments; students are advised to check the myPurdue Scheduling Assistant or contact the ECE Undergraduate Office for details. The Data Science program does not provide a standalone course listing, with relevant courses offered under the CS, STAT, and MATH prefixes. The Mathematics department's course schedule includes a variety of offerings, with multiple sections available for several courses. For specific course details, students should refer to the respective department websites.
