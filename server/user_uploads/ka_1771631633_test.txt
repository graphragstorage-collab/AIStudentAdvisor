CS47100: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (Spring 2026)

### Course Information
Artificial intelligence (AI) involves building intelligent machines capable of perceiving and acting rationally to achieve their goals. The course will cover the following topics:
- Search
- Constraint satisfaction
- Logic
- Reasoning under uncertainty
- Machine learning
- Planning

There will be four assignments consisting of both written and programming problems.

**Pre-requisites:**  
CS251 Data Structures (grade of C or better)

**Textbook:**  
S. Russell and P. Norvig (2020). *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach.* Pearson, 4th Edition. (ISBN: 9780134610993)  
*Note: The 3rd edition can also be used by finding the corresponding sections to read.*

### Policies
**Regrade Requests**  
After an assignment is graded, students have three days to request a regrade. After this period, the grade is finalized.

**Late & Absence Policy**  
A 10% penalty will be applied per day to late assignments. Assignments more than two days late will not be accepted. For consistency and fairness, absence requests should be made through the Office of the Dean of Students (ODOS). If ODOS gives the final decision to the instructor, then the request will be denied.

**Academic Honesty**  
Refer to Purdue's Student Guide for Academic Integrity. Academic dishonesty will result in a failing grade for the course and reporting. It is the student's responsibility to prevent others from copying their work.

**Accessibility**  
Purdue University strives to make learning as accessible as possible. If you experience physical or academic barriers based on disability, please contact the Disability Resource Center at: drc@purdue.edu or by phone at 765-494-1247 and the course instructor to arrange accommodations.

**Classroom Guidance Regarding Protect Purdue**  
Students who believe another person is threatening the safety of others by not complying with Protect Purdue protocols are encouraged to report the behavior to and discuss the next steps with their instructor. They can also report the behavior to the Office of the Student Rights and Responsibilities. See also Purdue University Bill of Student Rights and the Violent Behavior Policy under University Resources in Brightspace.

Please refer to additional university policies in BrightSpace.
