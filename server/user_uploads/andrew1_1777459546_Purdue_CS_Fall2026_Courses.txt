Purdue University Course Offerings for Fall 2026 in Computer Science

This document outlines the undergraduate and graduate course offerings for the Computer Science department at Purdue University, covering both the West Lafayette and Indianapolis campuses. The catalog includes a comprehensive list of courses such as CS 10100 Digital Literacy, CS 18000 Problem Solving and Object-Oriented Programming, and CS 50100 Computing for Science and Engineering, among others. Each course is accompanied by the instructor's name, providing essential information for students planning their academic schedules. The offerings encompass a range of topics, from foundational skills in programming and data science to advanced studies in artificial intelligence and cryptography, reflecting the department's commitment to providing a robust education in computer science.
