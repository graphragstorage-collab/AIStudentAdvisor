**Purdue University Data Science Program Overview**

The Data Science major at Purdue University combines computer science and statistics, preparing students for careers in various fields, including healthcare, security, and business. The program is jointly offered by the Department of Computer Science and the Department of Statistics, providing a comprehensive education in data science.
