### Homework Assignments
The course features a series of homework assignments, each with its own set of problems and exercises. The assignments are due on specific dates and times, and students are encouraged to submit their work through the Gradescope platform. The assignments cover a range of topics, including algorithm design, problem-solving strategies, and proofs of correctness.

Before submitting, we encourage you to ask yourself:
- Is this really the simplest way to solve the problem?
- Is this really the clearest way to explain the solution?
