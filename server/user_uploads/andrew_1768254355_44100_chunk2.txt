### Tentative Course Schedule for CS 44100 - Data Science Capstone

**Introduction (2 weeks)**:  
- Discuss CRISP-DM and its phases while selecting and settling on projects.

**Business and Data Understanding (4 weeks)**:  
- Explore projects and identify necessary information to address arising questions. Teams will plan their time schedule for the upcoming weeks. Reports and presentations will be required.

**Data Preparation (3 weeks)**:  
- Format data for analysis purposes. Reports and presentations will be required.

**Modeling (4 weeks)**:  
- Experiment with different modeling techniques using the team's data. Reports and presentations will be required.

**Evaluation (Remainder of Semester)**:  
- Assess the effectiveness of the model, possible success, and learning outcomes. Reports and presentations will be required.
