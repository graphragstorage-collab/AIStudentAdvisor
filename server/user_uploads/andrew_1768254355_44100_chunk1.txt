### Course Description  
This capstone course provides students with the opportunity to integrate their accumulated knowledge and skills to identify and solve realistic data science problems, focusing on practical applications. Projects may be sponsored by corporate partners or academic/non-academic research groups. Students will follow the six phases of the CRISP-DM (Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining) methodology for a team-based project, gaining experience in project planning, report writing, and presentations.

### Learning Objectives  
After successful completion of this course, students will be able to:  
- Identify the business objectives in a complex problem.  
- Develop appropriate data science goals and project plans.  
- Implement appropriate data pre-processing and analysis models.  
- Evaluate models and interpret results with respect to business objectives.  
- Present project outcomes through written reports and digital/oral presentations.  
(Note: "business" refers to the corporate partner or research group sponsoring the capstone projects.)

### Late Policy  
Late submissions incur a 5% penalty per 24-hour period and may be submitted up to three days late. Additional extensions may be granted at the discretion of the instructional staff if arranged in advance. Documentation is required for agreements regarding late submissions. Extensions exceeding one week are only granted for serious and documented emergencies, processed through the Dean of Students’ Office: [Purdue Absences](https://www.purdue.edu/advocacy/students/absences.html).

### Regrade Requests  
Requests for regrades must be made within seven days of receiving the grade, specifying what needs reassessment and why. Generic requests may be dismissed. No regrading will occur after this period.
